## Outside the Office

When I step away from research and teaching, I prioritize staying physically active, maintaining discipline through fitness, and enjoying good food.

### Weight Training & Calisthenics
* **Dedicated Strength Routine**: Strength training is my core habit for physical discipline and mental clarity. Even during demanding academic weeks, I make sure to get in at least a couple of focused sessions to reset.
* **Pull-Ups**: My favorite movement is the strict pull-up—focusing on uncompromising form with a complete dead-hang at the bottom and pulling all the way to maximum chest-to-bar height at the top.

### Cycling & Hill Climbing
* **Fondness for Climbs**: While I cycle less frequently these days, I have always had a strong passion for hill climbing and sustained elevation challenges.
* **Favorite Routes in Seoul**: When I was based in Seoul, my go-to cycling routes were the iconic uphill climbs of **Namsan Tower (Namsan Mountain)** and **Bugaksan (Bukak Skyway)**, which offered both punishing ascents and rewarding views.
